Meet CRUSTIO

A pizza and burger house that feels like a night out

CRUSTIO is a growing fast-food brand from Multan. The menu runs from tikka, fajita and peri peri pizzas to double-layer zinger burgers, loaded fries and crispy pasta. The room is built for groups, birthdays and late dinners.
